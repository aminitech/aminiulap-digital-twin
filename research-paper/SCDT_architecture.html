Sovereign Cognitive Digital Twin — Reference Architecture

Blockchain-anchored, network-as-sensor data platform for climate-resilient island & archipelagic nations
Zoe Aiyanna M. Cayetano  ·  George M. Gichuru  —  Amini Labs, Nairobi, Kenya

Sensing data captured from the network itself (6G ISAC / Network-as-a-Sensor) plus satellite, IoT, and edge devices is turned into spatio-temporal data cubes by Amini's GeoPackage engine, sharded and distributed across a network of Amini nodes (cell towers, modular data centers, servers, laptops), and anchored on Amini Chain for content-hashing, provenance, and node attestation. A zero-trust integrity spine treats every provider as untrusted by default yet enforces source quality — only verified, integrity-checked cubes enter the national sovereign data lake that powers the Sovereign Cognitive Digital Twin, whose orchestration closes the loop back to the sensing network.
