Sovereign Cognitive Digital Twin

Blockchain-anchored, network-as-sensor data platform for climate-resilient island & archipelagic nations.

Zoe Aiyanna M. Cayetano . George M. Gichuru - Amini Labs, Nairobi

Sensing captured from the network itself (6G ISAC / Network-as-a-Sensor) plus satellite, IoT and edge devices is turned into spatio-temporal data cubes by Amini's GeoPackage engine, sharded across a network of Amini nodes (cell towers, modular data centers, servers, laptops), and anchored on Amini Chain for content-hashing, provenance and node attestation. A zero-trust integrity spine treats every provider as untrusted by default yet enforces source quality - only verified, integrity-checked cubes enter the national sovereign data lake that powers the twin, whose orchestration closes the loop back to the sensing network.
