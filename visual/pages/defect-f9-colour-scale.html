Why this mattered. The paper places these two figures adjacent with a caption inviting the reader to compare them. Both were rendered with the same colourmap over the same 80 dB span, but each stage picked its own vmax from the 99th percentile of its own data and set vmin = vmax − 80. So the ramps are interchangeable to the eye and are not interchangeable in dB. Every by-eye comparison of hue between these two panels was off by the offset shown on the left.

What is fixed, and what is not. Both stages now resolve a shared scale per figure group, quantised to a 5 dB grid, first-writer-wins, with ULAP_PG_VMIN / ULAP_PG_VMAX overrides; each figure prints the scale it is on and its saturation fraction. Colour is now comparable. Spatial resolution still is not — these two figures are on 5 m and 8 m grids (audit § Outstanding, item 3).
